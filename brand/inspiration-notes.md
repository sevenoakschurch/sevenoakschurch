## What they have in common

1. **A short, confident hero line.** One sentence of vision over a big photo or video: "Light for the city & beyond" (Gas Street), "A home in the heart of King's Cross" (KXC).
2. **Same core navigation.** Both lead with *I'm New → Sundays → What's on → Give*, with *About* one level down. **Give is always in the top bar.**
3. **The homepage is a signpost, not a brochure.** After the hero comes one belief statement, then three cards or blocks: *I'm New*, *Plan your visit / Sundays*, *What's coming up*. Each has one button.
4. **Warm, plain, second-person copy.** "We can't wait to meet you!", "you are so welcome here!", "All welcome." Sentences are short with no church jargon up front.
5. **The newcomer is named directly.** Both list the kinds of people who might be arriving: "Whether you're new to London, exploring faith, or looking for a church home…", "visiting, exploring faith for the first time, returning to church…"
6. **FAQs as accordions** (click a question, the answer drops open) on the welcome, Sundays and giving pages. They answer: What's it like? What should I wear? Kids? Parking? Accessibility?
7. **Real people in the photos.** Candid, warm, crowd-level shots: people laughing, talking over coffee, worshipping. No stock images, no empty buildings.
8. **Giving is explained gently and practically.** One sentence of *why* (a response to God's generosity), then the *ways* (online, bank transfer, Gift Aid), then FAQs.
9. **Lots of white space and one idea per section.** Big headings, short paragraphs, a single clear button.
10. **A tidy footer** with safeguarding, privacy, complaints and charity number: important trust signals for UK churches.

## What stands out about each

### Gas Street – bold, minimal, editorial
- **Look:** near-black and off-white sections alternate. A large modern sans-serif (Geist) in tight, heavy headlines. Thin outlined buttons. No decoration; the photography does all the work.
- **Home:** a full-screen video hero with two buttons (*Plan a visit*, *Join online*), plus small "fact" captions underneath ("One church, four locations / Birmingham, United Kingdom").
- **I'm New:** "Hello & Welcome", a welcome video from the senior pastors, a "Next steps" list (gather, register, Alpha, community, serve), FAQs, and an "I said yes" section for people who have just made a faith decision.
- **Sundays:** a centred headline ("Sundays at Gas Street"), a strip of six photos side by side, then location cards showing **time • time • time** and the address. "What to expect" FAQs sit on a dark background.
- **Give:** a split layout, with the heading and button on the left and an accordion of ways to give on the right ("Give Online", "Bank Transfer", "Mobile App", "Gift Aid"). A photo banner reads "Your giving makes a difference", followed by 12 FAQs and a **financial snapshot** with income/expenditure pie charts. Very transparent.
- **About:** the vision statement, four one-word values (*Expectant, Generous, Alive, Real*), and the current year's vision theme.
- **Floating "Take your next step here" button** in the corner, always available.

### KXC – warm, classic, welcoming
- **Look:** a white background, a black circular logo, ALL-CAPS geometric headings (Gotham) and **one accent colour (gold)** used for every button and link.
- **Home:** the headline sits over a photo of people worshipping. A two-line welcome follows, then **zig-zag rows** (photo left, text right, then swapped), with a word like "SUNDAYS" or "I'M NEW" overlaid on the photo itself. A mailing list signup ("All Things News") comes last.
- **Sundays:** **service times on the homepage itself**. The Sundays page gives each service its time, venue and a one-line description of who it suits (kids' work, youth, translation, livestream). Kids & Youth gets its own block. Its FAQs are very practical ("Which service should I go to if I'm visiting?", "Where can I park?", "Will there be cameras?").
- **I'm New:** a "Get connected" form, a **Newcomers Meal** invitation, Alpha and other pathways, and FAQs.
- **Give:** short and simple: why, ways to give (bank, card, overseas), and a clear plain-English **Gift Aid explainer** ("25p for every £1").
- **About (Story & Beliefs):** the church's founding story told as a narrative, the vision, five core convictions as a short list, and a racial justice commitment.

## How we could apply this to our five pages

Brand rules come from `CLAUDE.md`: Teal and Oat lead, Periwinkle and Stone support, Mint Blue only as a sparing accent on Teal, and text in brand Black `#1a1a1a`. Fonts are Work Sans throughout, with Josefin Sans (wide letter spacing) for graphical headlines only. The oak grain pattern acts as a subtle texture bleeding off section edges.

### Site-wide
- **Navigation:** *Welcome · Sundays · Who we are · Give*, with "Give" as a button-style link. It should be short like both sites; we have fewer pages, which is a strength.
- **Buttons:** one consistent style, like KXC's gold: solid **Teal with Oat text** on light sections and **Oat with Teal text** on Teal sections. Mint Blue can be the hover/focus highlight on Teal sections only.
- **Section rhythm:** alternate **Oat** and **Teal** backgrounds, our version of Gas Street's light/dark rhythm, but warmer. Use White occasionally for a clean break.
- **Oak grain:** place it in a corner of the hero and one or two other sections, bleeding off the edge at ~30% Teal. Never behind body text. This is our distinctive touch; neither inspiration site has a pattern like it.
- **Headings:** Work Sans Medium for everyday headings. Josefin Sans, widely spaced, for the one or two "big moment" lines per page, especially over photos (our version of KXC's word-over-photo tiles).
- **FAQs:** accordion style on Welcome, Sundays and Give, using Stone for the divider lines.
- **Footer:** Teal background with Oat text, holding the address, service time, safeguarding, privacy, charity number and social links.

### 1. Landing page (home)
- **Hero:** a full-width photo of the church family with a short vision line in **Josefin Sans** layered on top, plus two buttons: *Plan your visit* and *Watch online* (if we stream).
- **Sunday times right under the hero** (KXC's best idea, and simple for a single-site church), e.g. "Sundays · 10:30am · [venue], Sevenoaks".
- **A one-sentence belief statement** on Oat, with oak grain in the corner.
- **Three signpost cards:** *New here?* → Welcome, *Sundays* → Sunday meetings, *Who we are* → Who we are.
- **A giving strip** (Teal background) with one sentence and a *Give* button.
- **Optional:** a newsletter signup.

### 2. Welcome
- Open with "You're so welcome", naming the people who might be arriving (new to Sevenoaks, exploring faith, returning to church, looking for a church home).
- A short welcome from the leaders (a photo or short video plus a few lines).
- **Next steps** as a simple list or cards: come on a Sunday, a newcomers' lunch/meal, an Alpha-style course, small groups, serving.
- A "Get connected" form or link.
- FAQs.

### 3. Sunday meetings
- Headline: "Sundays at Sevenoaks Church".
- A **time, venue and address card**, with a map link and parking notes. Gas Street's "time • time" style works well.
- **What to expect** in plain language: length, music, talk, coffee afterwards.
- **Kids & youth** block (KXC style).
- **Accessibility**, and the online/livestream option if we have one.
- A strip of candid photos (Gas Street style) to show what Sundays feel like.
- Practical FAQs: what to wear, parking, which door to use, kids.

### 4. Giving
- One or two sentences on *why we give* (a gentle, grateful tone like both sites).
- **Ways to give** as an accordion: online, bank transfer (account details), standing order.
- A **Gift Aid explainer** in KXC's plain style ("adds 25p for every £1 at no cost to you") with a link to the declaration form.
- **Optional, later:** a simple "where your giving goes" snapshot (Gas Street style). If we add a chart, use brand colours only.
- FAQs.

### 5. Who we are
- **Our story** told as a short narrative (KXC style).
- **Vision** in one line, plus **values** as 3–5 single words in large type (Gas Street style). Good candidates for Josefin Sans.
- **Our leaders/team** with photos.
- **What we believe**: a short list, with a link to a fuller statement if wanted.
- Network or denomination affiliations, if any, with logos in the footer as KXC does.

## Content we'll need from the church
- Sunday time(s), venue address, parking and accessibility details
- A one-line vision statement and 3–5 values
- A short church story, and leader names, photos and a welcome message
- Giving details (online platform link, bank details, Gift Aid form)
- Photography: candid, warm photos of real people (the biggest single factor in both sites' feel)
- Logo files and tone-of-voice notes (already on the "still to collect" list in `CLAUDE.md`)
- Charity number and safeguarding/privacy policy links for the footer
